Frictionless Workflow

From 2D Photo to Live 3D Model in 3 Steps

No 3D modeling skills, Blender software, or expensive graphic designers required. DukaanSpatial automates the spatial pipeline for Indian retail.

Step 01

Upload Photos or 3D Asset

Upload standard product images taken from 4 angles with your smartphone, or import ready GLTF/GLB 3D CAD files directly into our browser studio.

Step 02

Customize Materials & Hotspots

Configure material finishes (metallic, matte, leather, fabric), set custom color variants, and add interactive feature callout pins on key components.

Copy our lightweight, responsive iframe embed code and paste it into Shopify, Dukaan, WooCommerce, WordPress, or custom HTML in 10 seconds.
