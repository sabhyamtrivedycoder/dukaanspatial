Why I Built DukaanSpatial

Democratizing spatial computing and photorealistic 3D product visualization for the millions of small merchants powering Indian commerce.

Bridging the gap between 2D photos and spatial reality

Growing up in Giridih, Jharkhand, and observing local commercial retail up close, I saw a consistent struggle: small shopkeepers and emerging Indian online brands make incredible, high-quality products, but on the internet they are trapped in flat, 2D photos taken on basic phone cameras.

When consumers shop on big global luxury portals, they can spin watches, sneakers, and glasses in full 3D, inspect texture down to the stitch, and preview how it fits in their room. But building a 3D web experience currently costs over INR 50,000 per product and requires specialized 3D studio software like Blender and complex shaders.

As a student programmer passionate about mathematics, computer science, and WebGL, I asked myself: Why should 3D spatial commerce be reserved only for Apple, Nike, or Lenskart? Why can't a footwear seller in Agra, a jeweler in Jaipur, or an electronics maker in Bengaluru have interactive 3D product showcases with one line of code?

That is why I created DukaanSpatial: India's first 1-click interactive 3D and Augmented Reality product showcase platform.

Principle 01

Zero App Downloads

Built 100% on browser-native WebGL and WebXR. Shoppers never have to download a heavy app to experience 3D or Augmented Reality.

Principle 02

1-Line Copy & Paste

Any Indian merchant can copy our responsive iframe snippet and embed full 3D interactive models into Shopify, Dukaan, or WordPress in 10 seconds.

Principle 03

Bharat-First Economics

Priced in Indian Rupees starting at INR 499/month via UPI auto-pay, making spatial technology accessible to independent retailers and bootstrapped founders.
